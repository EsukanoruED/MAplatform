One-sentence: a compact status chip — fitness outcomes, certificate validity, site coverage state.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>Fit for duty</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning" dot>Fit with restrictions</Badge>
<Badge tone="danger" dot>Not fit</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral">Expires in 12 days</Badge>
```

Fitness outcomes map to fixed tones: fit → success, restricted → warning, unfit → danger, pending → neutral.
